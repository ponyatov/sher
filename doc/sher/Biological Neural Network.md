# 2.1 Biological Neural Network

Our brain is a vast graph of interconnected [[neuron]]s, a vast biological neural
network. A neuron is just a cell that can accept signals, and based on its chemical
and geometrical properties, produce an output. There are roughly 100 billion neurons in the human brain, with trillions of connections between them.

cell composed of three main parts,
- the [[soma]] (cell body), 
- the [[dendrite]]s, and 
- the [[axon]]
